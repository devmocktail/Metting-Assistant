FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it use my microphone?
No. It only listens to what plays through your speakers — the other person on the call. Your own voice and microphone are never captured.
Can people see it on a screen share?
No. The overlay uses Windows content protection, so it's hidden from screen captures and recordings. The other side sees their own screen, not your helper.
Does my audio get uploaded anywhere?
No. The caption model runs on your PC. Audio and captions never leave your machine — there's no cloud transcription.
Which meeting apps does it work with?
Any of them — Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex and browser calls. It captures system audio, so there's nothing to connect or integrate.
How fast are the captions?
About a second behind live speech on a typical laptop, using an on-device model tuned for real-time use.
Do I need an internet connection?
Only once, to sign in and set up the on-device caption engine. After that, captions run fully offline on your machine.
What does it cost?
A 7-day free trial, then ₹999/month for Pro. Cancel anytime from your account.
How do I pay, and can I cancel?
Pro is billed monthly through Razorpay. You can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the period.
